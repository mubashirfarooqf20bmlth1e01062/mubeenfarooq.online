OUR PROCESS

A practical process from market definition to opportunity progression.

We align research and outreach support with your actual services, target district profile, geography and internal business development workflow.

Discovery & service alignment

Understand your offerings, delivery footprint, ideal customers and growth priorities.

Target market definition

Agree states, district types, service categories and account selection criteria.

District and account research

Identify relevant public districts, charter networks and education organizations.

Decision-maker mapping

Research relevant administrators, department leaders and public professional contact details.

Opportunity and procurement review

Review public RFPs, RFQs, vendor portals and other opportunity signals.

Outreach coordination

Coordinate personalized communication using client-approved messaging.

Response and pipeline tracking

Log activity, responses, meetings, next steps and opportunity stages.

Reporting and refinement

Review findings and refine the target market and workflow with your team.

What we need from your team.

Service capabilities

What you provide, where you can deliver and relevant eligibility constraints.

Ideal district profile

Target states, district types, student populations or departments of interest.

Approved communication

Company positioning, outreach approvals, CRM preferences and internal owners.
