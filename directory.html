OUR VERIFICATION STANDARD

Accuracy before volume.

We are building this directory carefully. A district must first be confirmed as a U.S. public school district using an official district or government source. Professional names, roles, public work emails and office phone numbers are checked separately against official district staff directories or department pages.

01 / DISTRICT STATUS

Officially confirmed

Only public school districts supported by official district or government evidence qualify for inclusion.

02 / CONTACT DETAILS

Verified individually

Each professional contact field must be supported by a current official source. Unverified details are not displayed.

Sources and review dates

Published records will include source links and a last-verified date wherever available.
