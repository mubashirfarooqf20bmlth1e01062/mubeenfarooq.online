PRIVACY NOTICE

How Research Ops handles website inquiries.

This notice explains the basic information submitted through this website and how it is used to respond to business inquiries.

Information you submit

If you use the contact form, you may provide your name, company, business email, website, industry, requested support, target states and message. Please do not submit student records, patient information, protected health information, credentials or other confidential personal data.

How information is used

Information submitted through the form is used to review and respond to your business inquiry, understand the requested scope and communicate about potential services. The form is processed by FormSubmit and routed to the Research Ops contact inbox configured for this website.

Third-party services and links

This website may load fonts and images from third-party services and link to external websites. Those services may process technical information under their own privacy notices. Research Ops does not control external websites.

Retention and questions

Inquiry information may be retained for business communication and recordkeeping. To ask about a website inquiry, contact mubeen@mubeenfarooq.online.

This is a basic website privacy notice, not legal advice. Review it against the actual tools, data practices and laws applicable to your business before relying on it as a formal privacy policy.
