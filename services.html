OUR SERVICES

School district research, outreach & partnership development.

Focused business-development support for speech therapy staffing companies and school-based education service providers seeking to grow their U.S. district relationships.

How Research Ops supports your growth

Our services are designed around the district partnership journey—from finding the right school systems to organizing outreach and supporting the next steps in a potential contract relationship.

01

School District Research & Identification

Identify U.S. public school districts, charter networks and education agencies that align with your therapy staffing company's service areas and growth goals.

State and district targeting · district profiles · opportunity mapping
02

District Decision-Maker Research

Research relevant district leaders and teams, including Special Education, Student Services, Related Services and procurement contacts.

Role mapping · professional contact research · source tracking
03

SLP Staffing Opportunity Research

Find public signals of speech-language pathology staffing needs, such as district job postings, service gaps, vendor notices and procurement opportunities.

SLP roles · vacancy signals · contract research
04

School District Outreach Campaigns

Coordinate tailored B2B outreach to relevant district contacts on behalf of school-based therapy staffing providers.

Prospect segmentation · personalized outreach · campaign tracking
05

Follow-Up & Relationship Management

Maintain organized follow-up schedules, response tracking and next-step records so promising district conversations do not get lost.

Follow-up sequences · response logging · CRM updates
06

Partnership Development Support

Help move qualified conversations forward between staffing providers and school districts, supporting introductions, meeting coordination and opportunity tracking.

Meeting support · stakeholder coordination · opportunity progression
07

Vendor & Contract Opportunity Research

Research district vendor onboarding, procurement pages, RFPs, RFQs and public bid opportunities relevant to therapy staffing.

Vendor portals · RFP/RFQ monitoring · submission timelines
08

CRM & Pipeline Management

Build and maintain a clean business-development pipeline covering target districts, contacts, outreach, replies and opportunity stages.

CRM organization · pipeline hygiene · reporting
09

Multi-State Market Expansion Research

Map districts across selected U.S. states and regions to help staffing companies prioritize expansion and outreach.

State-by-state mapping · territory lists · market prioritization
10

Related School-Based Staffing Market Research

Research adjacent service lines offered by your company, such as occupational therapy, physical therapy, school psychology, SLPA and special education staffing.

Service-line research · district fit · competitor landscape

OUR ROLE

Your research and outreach partner—not the staffing provider.

Research Ops supports market intelligence and business development. Our staffing partners remain responsible for recruiting, credentialing, employing or contracting clinicians, clinical supervision and delivering services under their agreements.

Common target roles researched

Special Education Directors · Directors of Student Services · Related Services Coordinators · Procurement and vendor management contacts · District administrators.

Potential service-line markets

Speech-Language Pathology (SLP) · Speech-Language Pathology Assistants (SLPA) · Occupational Therapy · Physical Therapy · School Psychology · Special Education support, where offered by the partner.
