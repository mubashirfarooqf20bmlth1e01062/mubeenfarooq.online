ABOUT RESEARCH OPS

Helping therapy staffing companies connect with school district opportunities.

Research-led business development support for companies serving U.S. schools.

FOUNDER

Mubeen Farooq

Founder, Research Ops

Research Ops is led by Mubeen Farooq and focuses on research, prospect identification and business-development support for companies operating in the school-based therapy and education staffing market.

Our work begins by understanding a partner company's actual staffing services and target territories. We then research relevant school districts, identify appropriate professional contacts, organize outreach and follow-up, and help maintain a clear view of developing partnership opportunities.

We believe effective business development starts with accurate research, relevant communication and consistent execution—not generic bulk prospect lists.

OUR COMMITMENT

Relevant research. Respectful outreach. Clear reporting.

We aim to provide structured research and dependable business-development support while respecting district procurement processes and each partner's service capabilities.
