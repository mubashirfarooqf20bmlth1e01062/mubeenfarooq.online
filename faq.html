FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers about school district business development support.

Learn what Research Ops does, which organizations we support and how research and outreach projects work.

What is Research Ops?

Research Ops is a School District Business Development Agency. We support companies serving K–12 education with market research, district identification, decision-maker intelligence, procurement research, outreach coordination and pipeline support.

Who does Research Ops work with?

Potential clients include school-based therapy providers, special education organizations, education staffing firms, behavioral health providers, school psychology groups, EdTech companies, curriculum vendors and other school-facing businesses.

Does Research Ops provide speech therapy or employ SLPs?

No. Research Ops provides business development research and coordination. The client or contracted service provider is responsible for recruiting, employing or contracting licensed professionals, clinical supervision and delivering services.

Can you research all 50 states?

We can scope public-source district research across U.S. states. Actual target markets should reflect the client's service capabilities, licensing, staffing, contracting eligibility and priorities.

Can you identify Special Education Directors and SLP Coordinators?

We can research relevant public professional roles and contact information using official district websites and appropriate public sources. Availability and accuracy vary by district, and records should be source-checked.

Do you find school district RFPs and vendor registration opportunities?

We can research public RFPs, RFQs, invitations to bid, procurement calendars, vendor portals and registration instructions, then organize relevant details for client review.

Can you guarantee meetings or signed district contracts?

No. Outcomes depend on the client's offer, pricing, credentials, market fit, procurement rules, timing and district decisions. We support the research and coordination process, not guaranteed outcomes.

Do you send outreach on behalf of clients?

Outreach scope, sender identity, messaging, approvals and applicable communication rules are agreed with the client before activity begins.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on states or districts, depth of contact research, procurement monitoring, outreach coordination and reporting frequency. We discuss scope before proposing a fee.

Can you work with our CRM?

We can discuss working within your existing CRM or an agreed spreadsheet workflow, depending on access, permissions and scope.

How do you handle student or patient information?

District business development research should not require identifiable student or patient records. Do not submit confidential student, patient or protected health information through the website contact form.

How do we get started?

Use the contact form to describe your company, service lines, target states and goals. We can then discuss fit, scope, deliverables and next steps.
